I build research software at the intersection of computational biology, LLM systems, and developer tooling.

I am **Tang Ziya (唐梓涯)**. `Tang` is my family name, `Ziya` is my given name, and `tcztzy` is the initialism of **天才之唐梓涯 (Genius Tang Ziya)**.

I care about software that makes scientific work more legible, reproducible, and composable.

- Working on agent skills, MCP servers, model-serving infrastructure, and scientific reasoning workflows.
- Interested in research engineer, ML systems, and computational biology opportunities in Europe, the UK, and the US.
- Usually shipping with Python, Rust, Jupyter, Nix, `uv`, and GPU/HPC-heavy workflows.
